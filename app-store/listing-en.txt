App name: Lithos
Subtitle: Gem catalog & crystal games
Primary category: Education
Secondary category: Games

Description:
Lithos is a field catalog of gemstones plus a whole arcade of crystal games — all in one expedition.

• Browse hundreds of gems with photos, Mohs hardness, chemistry, and origins
• Learn geology with lessons and the Rock Teacher
• Play Morph Hunt, Gem Factory, Night Shift, Gem Arena, Battlefield, and more
• Scavenger hunts, quizzes, and member classes

Built for curious explorers and young geologists. Parent supervision recommended for chat features.
