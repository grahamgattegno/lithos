Lithos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Lithos is an educational gemstone catalog and games experience. This policy describes how the Lithos app and website handle information.

What we collect

  • On your device: Game progress, settings, and chat messages you type may be stored locally in your browser or app (localStorage / on-device storage). Morph Hunt test chat can stay on your device and other tabs on the same device unless you use a separate chat server.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

If you use optional features (for example AI Rock Teacher with an API key configured by the operator, YouTube embeds on Learn pages, or affiliate links to gem shops), those services have their own policies. Only enable keys and links you trust.

Children

Lithos is designed for learning. Parents and guardians should supervise online chat and purchases.

Contact

Questions: use the contact options on grahamgattegno.github.io/lithos or your app support email listed on the App Store listing.
