ALLUREZ Affiliate Programme

Partner with Allurez, a leader in NYC-made fine jewelry known for exceptional craftsmanship, ethical sourcing, and timeless design. We offer luxury but also value as all jewelry comes straight from the source with no middleman markups. Our collection spans engagement rings, wedding bands, personalized jewelry, and one-of-a-kind pieces handcrafted to celebrate life’s most meaningful moments. With an average order value of over $2,000, our program offers strong earning potential for publishers, creators, and luxury-focused content partners.

As an Allurez affiliate, you’ll earn 1–15% commissions on qualifying sales. Please note that commissions are not paid on refunded or fraudulent orders, and we require verifiable links to the content driving traffic and conversions.

To maintain brand integrity, affiliates may not advertise coupon codes or bid on Allurez brand terms in Google Ads or any paid search campaigns.

Join us in sharing fine jewelry that customers treasure for a lifetime — and earn meaningful revenue while doing it.
